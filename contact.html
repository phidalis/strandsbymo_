Quick Answers

All FAQs
How soon will my order arrive?

Strandsbymo_ orders placed before 2pm are dispatched the same day and typically arrive within 24 hours. UK delivery takes 1–3 business days.

Can I visit the studio before buying?

Absolutely. Our London studio is open Tuesday to Saturday by appointment. Book via WhatsApp and we will set aside your favourites to view.

Do you install wigs and braids?

Yes — wig installs, frontal melts, sew-ins and braiding are all available in-studio. Prices depend on the style and length; message us for a quote.

What if my order is wrong or damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours with photos and your order number. We will arrange a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.
