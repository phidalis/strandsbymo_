Strandsbymo_ began in a small London studio with three bundles and a big dream: to make premium, authentic human hair accessible to women across the UK without the guesswork, the long waits or the disappointment.

Today we are a full-service hair house — sourcing virgin and raw hair, hand-finishing lace wigs, braiding in our studio and formulating our own care line. What has never changed is our obsession with quality and the way a great install can make a woman stand a little taller.

Every strand we sell is inspected by hand. Every order is packed with care. Every client is treated like a friend who borrowed our favourite lipstick.
